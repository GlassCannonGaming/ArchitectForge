 Standard Items located in a all appartment buildings
 
 
*****Standard Items in All Apartment Buildings

• Front door intercom system
• Individual mailboxes
• Fire extinguishers
• Smoke detectors
• Exit signs
• Concrete or carpeted stairwells
• Elevators
• Shared laundry room
• Central HVAC vents or radiators
• Utility meters
• Trash chutes or dumpsters
• Hallway lighting fixtures
• Breaker panels

*****Specific Items in 1970s and 1980s Apartment Buildings

• Popcorn ceilings
• Dark wood wall paneling
• Avocado green, harvest gold, or almond-coloured kitchen appliances
• Shag carpeting
• Linoleum or patterned vinyl flooring
• Formica countertops with faux wood trim
• Brass track lighting
• Plastic faux-wicker dome lamps hanging from gold chains
• Textured amber or brown glass lighting fixtures
• Wall-mounted rotary or early touch-tone phones
• Dial thermostats
• Louvred bi-fold closet doors
• Hollywood regency-style bulb vanity mirrors
• Brown, beige, or burnt orange bathroom tile




*****Living Room & Shared Areas

• Television
• Remote controls
• Sofa or couch
• Coffee table
• Books and magazines
• Throw pillows and blankets
• Houseplants
• Wall art or framed photos
• Floor lamps
• Area rugs
• Wi-Fi router
• Coasters
• Board games or video game consoles

***Unkempt Version:

**Living Room & Shared Areas

• Cathode-ray tube (CRT) television flickering with gray static
• Stained, wood-grained TV remote with missing battery covers
• Sagging tweed sofa with frayed armrests and deep cigarette burns
• Water-ringed laminate coffee table cluttered with overflowing glass ashtrays
• Yellowed, dog-eared paperback thrillers and torn true-crime magazines
• Muted, threadbare throw blankets smelling faintly of mildew
• Half-dead, drooping pothos plant in a cracked plastic pot
• Dusty, lopsided framed landscape painting with a cracked glass pane
• Tarnished brass floor lamp with a crooked, heavily stained fabric shade
• Faded area rug worn down to the backing threads in heavy traffic areas
• Tangled nest of dusty black extension cords snaking along the baseboards




*****Kitchen & Dining

• Toaster or microwave

***Unkempt version:

***Kitchen & Dining

• Dented metal kettle covered in a layer of sticky, grease-fossilized dust
• Scorched two-slice toaster with a frayed, cloth-insulated power cord
• Scratched Teflon frying pan sitting in a sink full of murky, stagnant water
• Mismatched ceramic plates with hairline fractures and tea-stained mugs
• Dull, plastic-handled steak knives and bent aluminum forks
• Rusted wire dish rack holding a lone, bone-dry water glass
• Overflowing plastic trash bin with a stained, swinging lid
• Sun-bleached refrigerator magnets holding up faded, peeling takeout menus
• Unraveling roll of generic paper towels sitting directly on a sticky counter
• Singed, greasy oven mitt resting precariously near the stovetop burner
• Cloudy tupperware containers with warped, non-matching lids
• Grimy spice rack holding jars of greyish, expired powders
• Dried-out, crumbling kitchen sponge sitting in a pool of crusty dish soap
